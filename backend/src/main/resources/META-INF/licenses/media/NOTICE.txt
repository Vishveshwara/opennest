OpenNest media decoder dependencies

OpenNest remains AGPL-3.0-only. These dependency grants do not change that license.

JavaCV and JavaCPP 1.5.14 are used under their Apache-2.0 option.
Their upstream dual-license text is retained in JavaCV-JavaCPP-LICENSE.txt.
Sources and copyright headers:
https://github.com/bytedeco/javacv/tree/1.5.14
https://github.com/bytedeco/javacpp/tree/1.5.14
https://github.com/bytedeco/javacpp-presets/tree/1.5.14/ffmpeg

FFmpeg 8.1.2-1.5.14 uses the standard non-GPL platform classifier.
Its executable reports LGPL version 3 or later; the LGPLv3 text and the GPLv3
terms it incorporates are retained alongside this notice. This dependency's
"or later" option applies to FFmpeg, not to OpenNest.
FFmpeg source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n8.1.2
Build configuration, component versions, source archives and patches:
https://github.com/bytedeco/javacpp-presets/blob/1.5.14/ffmpeg/cppbuild.sh
https://github.com/bytedeco/javacpp-presets/tree/1.5.14/ffmpeg

The upstream native build also links codec/support libraries with their own
licenses, identified by the pinned build configuration. Preserve component
notices and make the matching sources/build materials available when
redistributing native binaries. A binary release must include that material;
this repository change does not publish or deploy a release.
